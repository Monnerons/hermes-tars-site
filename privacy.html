Privacy policy

Hermes TARS is a private assistant used only by members of the Monneron household.

With the account holder's consent, it accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data of the household's own Google account, solely to perform tasks requested by household members.

Data is processed on a privately owned server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Hermes TARS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
